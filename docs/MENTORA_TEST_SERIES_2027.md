# MENTORA TEST SERIES — JEE 2027 (and Class 11 track for JEE 2028) — v2, 10 Oct 2026

**v2 change (founder):** full JEE Main mocks now start in **early December**, not January, and full JEE Advanced mocks start in **December** too. This leaves December and early January for course correction before the exam, in line with the major coaching test series.

**Exam anchors (verify before publishing):**
- **JEE Main 2027 Session 1:** tentatively **22–24 and 28–30 Jan 2027** (31 Jan buffer), per the NTA calendar reported 16 Sep 2026.
- **Session 2:** expected **April 2027** (dates TBA).
- **JEE Advanced 2027:** expected **May 2027**, organised by IIT Delhi (date TBA).

**Principles**
- **Every test's syllabus is published ≥ 2 weeks before the test.** Meera Ma'am turns it into a revision plan (revision classes + DPPs on those chapters).
- **Phase order:** part tests (Nov) → full Main mocks (Dec–Jan) → a taper week before the exam → Advanced build-up (Feb–Mar) → Session 2 → Advanced mocks to the exam.
- **Last 4–5 days before each exam: no full mocks.** Students do light revision, their error log, formula sheets and short sectionals. Coaching practice is the same: rest and consolidate, don't test.
- **Chapter lists are mapped to the official syllabi** (JEE Main 2026/2027 and JEE Advanced 2026/2027) in `Mentora Exam Sources`. Topics dropped from JEE Main are excluded from Main-pattern tests. They are kept for Advanced only where the Advanced syllabus includes them.
- **"Not studied yet":** a Class 12 student can mark chapters their coaching hasn't finished. Those chapters still appear in full mocks (as in the real exam), but the analysis reports them separately, and Meera plans them instead of counting them as weaknesses.
- **Timing and access**
  - Main tests: Sundays, or Wednesdays where shown.
  - Each test is open 10:00 am – 10:00 pm, with a fixed "live" slot for ranking among MENTORA students.
  - Missed tests can be taken later (unranked).
  - Final rehearsal mocks run at the real exam time.
- **JEE Main pattern:** 75 questions (25 per subject: 20 MCQ + 5 numerical), 3 hours, 300 marks, +4/−1, all per the **latest official NTA bulletin**. Confirm whether numerical answers carry negative marking before launch.
- **JEE Advanced pattern:** Paper 1 + Paper 2, 3 hours each, with mixed question types including partial marking. Follow the most recent official papers, as the pattern changes every year.
- **After every test**
  - Full analysis: score, accuracy, time per question, attempt strategy (what was skipped and when), error types, chapter strengths and weaknesses, rank among MENTORA students.
  - The error log is updated.
  - A personalised re-test on weak areas within 7 days.
- **Every question is machine-verified before use** (computation + independent model + official keys where applicable), and every question has a "Report an error" button.

## The entry door: zero friction, then low cost

The plans page shows **three cards: Free · Complete (⭐ Recommended) · Test Series.** Free is not a purchase decision, so it doesn't add confusion. It is the main door in.

**1. MENTORA Free.** Sign up with a phone OTP. No payment, no card, free forever.
- Diagnostic test + personal chapter report + Meera's plan preview.
- **One "first class" with each teacher** (~25 min). These use specially prepared, pre-voiced trial lessons, so each free student costs only a few rupees.
- All **free All-India MENTORA Mocks** (6 Dec, and one free mock each month), with full analysis.
- A few doubts a day (photo / chat, short voice).
- Streaks and leaderboards. Hostel and coaching-centre leaderboards are a strong word-of-mouth driver.

**2. "Try Complete for 7 days" at a token price** (amount set later; something like ₹1–99).
- The full Complete experience, so the student builds the daily habit.
- The token price filters out fake and duplicate accounts and covers part of the cost.
- **No auto-renewal surprises:**
  - a clear reminder before the trial ends;
  - one-tap cancel;
  - at launch, no autopay at all. Parents trust this more, and it avoids the subscription traps that consumer-protection rules prohibit.

**3. Upgrade moments,** where students are invited to Complete or Test Series:
- after the diagnostic report;
- after each free class;
- after each free mock's analysis ("fix these 3 weak chapters with Kavya Ma'am");
- when the daily free doubts run out.

**Abuse control:** one free account per phone number (OTP), with device checks; trial classes limited to one per teacher.

## Why two plans, and why Complete is recommended
- **Two clear choices with one recommendation means no confusion.** The question for the student is simple: "Already in coaching and only want tests? Test Series. Otherwise, Complete."
- **The Test Series is the easier sale.** Students already buy test series every year, it's cheap for us to run (no live voice), and it brings coached students in. After every test it points them to Complete ("fix your weak chapters with Kavya Ma'am — try a class free").
- **Complete is the reason MENTORA exists,** and it's the one we recommend. It's a personal teacher every day for a fraction of offline coaching fees. Nothing is guaranteed to sell, so the Kanpur pilot and the first month of Early Access will show the real conversion.

**Removed, and why:**
- **Rankers:** the adaptive system already teaches at Advanced depth to strong students and students targeting Advanced, so a separate tier isn't needed.
- **Institutions:** not a plan. Pilot hostels and centres get Complete through access codes, arranged directly.

## Hybrid vs live: not a plan choice

| | Fully live (every sentence made fresh) | Hybrid (verified prepared explanations + live conversation) |
|---|---|---|
| **Feel** | Most natural | Natural if prepared parts are short and every student reply is handled live |
| **Correctness** | Risk of wrong numbers or explanations | Explanations verified in advance (computation + independent check) |
| **Speed** | Slower to start speaking | Prepared parts start instantly |
| **Board** | Drawn on the fly, quality varies | Diagrams prepared properly; live drawing still possible |
| **Cost per class-hour** | Measured ₹41 on 10 Oct (short class with many silences). A full teaching hour could reach ₹70–100+ | Estimate ₹20–35 (prepared speech is voiced once per chapter and shared by all students). **To be measured in W9** |
| **Work needed** | Little | A verified lesson plan for every chapter (W2) |

**Decision:** every class is hybrid, and that stays invisible to students. The teacher always listens and replies live; doubts are always fully live. Prepared parts stay short (about 30–40 s) and are always followed by a live check. "Live vs hybrid" is how we deliver classes, not something we sell.

## Cost guardrails (decided now, numbers later)
- **Average use is what matters.** Earlier estimate: about 7 class-hours per student per month, i.e. roughly ₹150–250 per student per month in hybrid. That is within a ₹499–599 price after GST and payment fees.
- **Heavy users (3 hours every day) would cost much more.** That is why classes are capped at up to 3 hours a day, and doubts are under fair use.
- **Long-term levers:**
  - Sarvam volume pricing;
  - a cheaper or self-hosted Indian TTS voice;
  - more of each class prepared and pre-voiced.
- **Prices are set after W9 measures the real hybrid cost** in the pilot.
